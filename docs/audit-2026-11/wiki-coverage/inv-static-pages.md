Area: every top-level page of the built static site, every section a belief page carries, topic pages (`t/`), contributor pages (`u/`), the `p/<key>.txt` printouts and the `data/` exports. Built site checked: revision 3276ea1 (2026-11-21), 7,123 pages under `p/` (each as `.html`, `.json`, `.txt`), 104 topic pages under `t/` (23 positional, 81 directory), 1 contributor page under `u/`, 14 files under `data/`.

Live wiki: the 13 pages cloned 2025-08-09. All 13 predate the published site. None of them names a page, list, section, export or contribution path on it. The only overlap is conceptual: the live pages describe scoring ideas (ReasonRank, EVS, equivalency, stability, media impact) that the site either runs differently or, in method.html's own words, "does not run" (method.py:225, the `#notrun` table).
