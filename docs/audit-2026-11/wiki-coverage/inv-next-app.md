# Inventory: the Next.js app against the GitHub wiki

Area: every route under `src/app` (pages and API), the belief page and its section components, every engine in `src/core/scoring/`, `src/lib/fallacy`, `src/lib/consensus.ts`, `src/features/legal-framework`, `prisma/schema.prisma`, and the `/algorithms` explainers.

Read this first. The Next.js app is **not published** (README.md:12, :76: "the older product, which is not published"). Nothing in this area is something a reader of myklob.github.io sees. "Wired" below therefore says **app** for anything a route, component or API in the running Next.js app reaches, and **unwired** for code nothing imports. Priority still follows the brief: High where the app shows it on the belief page or where it decides a number.

Search method: every feature was grepped in the 13 live pages (`scratchpad/wiki/*.md`) and the 39 drafts (`docs/wiki/*.md`) under several names (file name, function name, route, plain-English name). Matches that were only the common word (for example "leverage" or "standing" in ordinary prose) were checked by context and not counted.

**Headline.** The live wiki is 13 concept pages from August 2025. It names about a dozen scores and gives a formula or sketch for seven of them (ReasonRank, belief score, Truth Score, EVS, ECLS linkage, Confidence Stability, Equivalency, Media). Every one of those formulas differs from what `src/core/scoring/` computes. It names no route, no belief page section, no API, no guarantee, no CLI and no CI check. The October 2026 drafts in `docs/wiki/` cover most of the scoring engines and belief page sections correctly in substance, but almost nothing outside the belief page and the scores (markets, agent API, conversations, forum, battlefield, law, search, FAQ, problems pages, CLIs, schema).
