This template moved to media-analysis-template.html (ISE Media Analysis template v4, 2026-10-03). The beliefs a work carries and the quality of the work are now argued on one page per work, because the two questions share a Scorecard (Quality, Influence, Reach, Media Truth Score, Epistemic Impact) and a reader should not have to open a second page to learn whether a well-made work is also true. What this file used to hold is there in current form: the Scores at a Glance row became the Scorecard, the Beliefs Supports and Weakens tables became Beliefs This Work Carries (one row per claim, in positive form, ranked by epistemic impact), and the quality pro and con reasons became the Quality Arguments table. Directness of Advocacy was dropped as a multiplier; how a work carries a claim is now a description that points the centrality debate at the right evidence. This stub stays so old links do not break.
