Where a belief sits under Direction and Claim Strength is typed by its author: it says which way the belief points and how strongly it is worded, not how well it holds up. Importance is left off until something computes it.

What this page does. It gives every belief about this topic one fixed address, so the thousand ways of saying the same thing collapse into a single entry and the real reasons to agree or disagree can be counted and weighed by evidence instead of by how often they get repeated. The address has three parts, one per table below: Direction (which way it runs), Claim Strength (how absolute), and Specificity (where it sits on the general-to-specific tree). Same address means the same claim, so it merges; nearly the same address is flagged as a possible duplicate for someone to argue. Full logic lives on One Page Per Topic.

Continuum 3: General to Specific, with Branching Subcategories (General ↔ Specific)
